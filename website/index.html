Frequently Asked Questions

Everything shopkeepers ask about hardware, offline data, and printer compatibility.

Does KamaiPlus work when there is no internet connection? +
Yes! KamaiPlus is built with a local-first SQLite database architecture. Your items, prices, barcodes, customer khata, and billing counter function at 100% speed even if your shop has zero cellular network or Wi-Fi. When internet becomes available, data syncs safely to the cloud in the background.
Which thermal printers are compatible with KamaiPlus? +
KamaiPlus supports all standard 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) Bluetooth, USB, and Wi-Fi ESC/POS thermal printers (such as Everycom, NGX, POSIFLEX, Rugtek, TVS, Epson, and generic Bluetooth printers from Amazon).
How does KamaiPlus prevent billing calculation errors? +
Unlike other billing software that uses floating-point math (which causes rounding discrepancies like ₹0.10 or ₹0.20), KamaiPlus uses strict Integer Paise accounting (1 Rupee = 100 Paise). Every discount, tax, subtotal, and customer balance is exact to the nearest paisa.
What happens if I change or lose my phone? +
Your data is continuously encrypted and backed up to Google Firebase Cloud. Whenever you install KamaiPlus on a new phone and sign in with your verified phone number, your entire product catalogue, khata balances, and past bills restore automatically in seconds.
Where can I find step-by-step help and guides? +
Visit our dedicated KamaiPlus Help & Documentation Center. It contains step-by-step guides with deep permalinks for printer setup, barcode scanning, vertical workflows, and bilingual (English/Hindi) support.
