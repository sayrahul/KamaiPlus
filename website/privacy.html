Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 • Application: KamaiPlus (com.kamaiplus.pos)

KamaiPlus ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of retail merchants, business owners, and their customers. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related web services collect, process, and protect your information.

1. Offline-First Architecture & Data Ownership

KamaiPlus operates primarily on an offline-first architecture. All your product inventories, customer credit records, supplier contacts, and sales transaction bills are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database. You retain 100% ownership and control of your business data.

2. Information We Collect & Why

When you use KamaiPlus, we may collect the following categories of information to provide and enhance our services:

  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number and store name for authentication and OTP verification.
  • Store Transaction Metadata: Item names, sales volumes, bill counts, and monetary figures stored in Integer Paise for billing and reporting.
  • Customer Khata Records: Customer names, contact numbers, and transaction balances entered manually by the merchant for credit ledger tracking.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

To deliver essential point-of-sale functionality, the app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely for real-time barcode scanning to add items to bills or inventory. No photos or video feeds are recorded or stored on remote servers.
  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required to discover and connect with ESC/POS 58mm and 80mm wireless thermal receipt printers.
  • Storage & Documents: Required to export GST invoices and sales reports in PDF or CSV formats to your device's download folder.

4. Cloud Synchronization & Security

When internet connectivity is detected, your data is securely synchronized with Google Cloud Firebase using SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption. This ensures that your business records are never lost in case your mobile device is damaged, lost, or upgraded.

5. No Sale of Personal or Business Data

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your store data, customer list, or sales volumes to any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Your financial and store data remains confidential to your business account.
