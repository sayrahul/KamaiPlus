POS Counter

How to Create Fast Counter Bills in 3 Seconds

KamaiPlus is designed for rush hours. You can add items via instant barcode scan, tap favorites, or search loose items.

Step 1: Open POS Billing Tab

Tap the elevated green Billing button in the bottom center of your navigation bar.

Step 2: Add Items to Cart

Either point your camera/scanner at any barcode, or tap the item card from the quick grid. Use +/- buttons to adjust quantity.

Step 3: Fast Tender Selection & 1-Tap Print

Tap cash chips (₹100, ₹200, ₹500) to see change due, or tap 'Exact/UPI'. Tap 'Charge & Print' to print receipt in 0.8 seconds.

Hardware Setup

How to Connect a Bluetooth Thermal Printer (58mm / 80mm)

KamaiPlus supports all standard ESC/POS Bluetooth and USB receipt printers without any drivers or third-party apps.

1. Pair Printer in Android Bluetooth Settings

Turn on your thermal printer. Open your phone's Settings > Bluetooth, search for the printer (e.g. MPT-II, POS-58, Everycom), and enter PIN 0000 or 1234.

2. Select Printer in KamaiPlus App

Open KamaiPlus > Tap Menu (bottom right) > Select Printer Settings. Choose your paired printer from the dropdown list and select 58mm or 80mm paper size.

3. Test Print Receipt

Tap the 'Print Test Receipt' button. If the paper feeds and prints the store header, your setup is complete!

⚠️ Printer Troubleshooting:
  • Ensure Location/Nearby Devices permission is granted to KamaiPlus in Android App Info.
  • The thermal paper roll is inserted upside down. Flip the paper roll around.
Inventory Master

Managing Products, Barcodes & Low Stock Alerts

Add items with purchase price, selling price, tax rate, and barcode. KamaiPlus automatically deducts stock upon every bill.

How to Add an Item with Barcode

Go to Products Tab > Tap the '+ Add Product' button. Tap the Camera icon next to Barcode field to scan product packet, enter Item Name, Selling Price, and current stock.

Low Stock Radar Alerts

Set a 'Minimum Stock Level' (e.g. 5 units). When inventory dips below this threshold, KamaiPlus flags the item in yellow so you can reorder before stock runs out.

Credit Ledger

Customer Khata (Udhar) & WhatsApp Payment Reminders

Never lose a single rupee to forgotten udhar. Track customer debit/credit transactions with exact date, time, and bill references.

Billing on Credit (Udhar Bill)

In POS Billing, tap 'Select Customer'. Choose or add customer name & phone number. In payment tender, select 'Udhar / Credit'. The bill amount is instantly added to their ledger.

Sending WhatsApp Reminder with UPI Link

Go to Khata Tab > Tap the customer > Tap the green 'WhatsApp Reminder' button. An automated, polite message with their exact outstanding balance and your store UPI QR link is sent in 1 tap.

Customized Retail Workflows

Vertical-Specific Workflows & Best Practices

🛒 1. Kirana & General Store Workflow

Loose Items: Add items with unit 'kg', 'g', or 'litre'. At checkout, enter weight (e.g. 0.75 for 750 grams) and the app computes exact paise price without rounding loss.

👗 2. Garments & Apparel Workflow

Variants & Exchange: Under a style (e.g. Denim Jeans), create Size variants (30, 32, 34, 36). For exchanges, add returned size with negative quantity or use 'Exchange' mode on the billing screen.

💊 3. Pharmacy & Medical Store Workflow

Batch & Expiry: When adding medicines, enter Batch No and Expiry MM/YY. The POS warns the cashier if an item has expired and prevents illegal sale automatically.

🔧 4. Hardware & Electrical Store Workflow

B2B GST Invoices: Select customer with registered GSTIN. KamaiPlus formats formal Tax Invoices with HSN code, CGST, SGST, IGST, and prints on 3-inch or A4 format.

☕ 5. Cafe & Fast Food Workflow

KOT Printing: When order is confirmed, tap 'Print KOT' to print kitchen preparation slips while keeping bill open until customer checkout.

Data Safety

How Offline Mode & Automatic Cloud Sync Works

KamaiPlus stores all shop data directly inside your Android device in an ultra-fast SQLite database. This means zero loading spinners and zero dependence on cell reception.

Automatic Background Cloud Sync

As soon as your phone connects to Wi-Fi or cellular network, KamaiPlus securely uploads bill records and stock changes to Google Cloud Firebase. You do not need to press any manual sync button.

Restoring Data on a New Phone

Download KamaiPlus on your new phone and sign in with your registered phone number. Tap 'Restore Shop Data' and your entire business history will appear on your new screen in seconds.
